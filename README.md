# 🛒 Ecommerce Sales & Profit Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project features an interactive **Power BI Ecommerce Sales Dashboard** built to track, analyze, and visualize business performance. The primary objective is to monitor key business metrics such as overall sales, total profits, quantities sold, and profitability across various segments to drive data-backed business decisions.

## 💾 Data Source & Preparation
* **Data Source:** Leveraged dummy ecommerce dataset downloaded from **Kaggle**.
* **Data Cleaning & Transformation:** Utilized **Power Query Editor** to handle data transformations, manage data types, and ensure data integrity before modeling.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Features
* **Tool:** Power BI Desktop
* **Data Transformation:** Power Query
* **Calculations (DAX):** Developed custom **DAX Measures** to calculate critical business KPIs including:
  * `Sum of Amount` (Total Revenue)
  * `Sum of Profit` (Total Profit)
  * `Sum of Quantity` (Total Units Sold)

## 📊 Visualizations Implemented
The dashboard uses a variety of advanced charts to provide clear and actionable insights:
* **KPI Cards:** For instant visibility into Total Revenue (\$438K), Total Profit (\$37K), and Total Quantity (6K).
* **Clustered Column / Bar Charts:** To analyze the **Sum of Amount by State** (tracking top states like Maharashtra and Madhya Pradesh) and **Sum of Profit by Sub-Category** (Printers, Bookcases, etc.).
* **Donut Chart:** To show the **Sum of Quantity by Category** (Clothing, Electronics, Furniture).
* **Pie Chart:** To display the **Sum of Quantity by Payment Mode** (COD, UPI, Debit Card).
* **Line / Area / Trend Chart:** For **Sum of Profit by Month** to identify seasonal trends and profit fluctuations throughout the year (January to December).
